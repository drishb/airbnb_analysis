DECISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The req-43 model stays the PRIMARY reported model. The 263-dummy fixed-effects
variant fits better (R^2 0.330 -> 0.481, adjusted 0.329 -> 0.477), which says
neighbourhood location carries real price signal beyond the base model's
regional control. But it cannot be the reported model: 7 neighbourhoods have a
single listing, giving those rows leverage 1 once their dummy enters the
design, which makes every HC3 standard error in the fit infinite. Requirement
44 mandates HC3, so the FE spec is inadmissible here. The small neighbourhoods
that the n>=100 rule already excludes from aggregation also break the listing-
level model. The FE result is retained below as a robustness note only.
